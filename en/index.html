Frequently asked questions

Why wasn’t my story created? Every story goes through a safety check. If the topic is not found suitable for a bedtime story, or something goes wrong while it is being prepared, the story is not created and you are not charged a story. You can change the topic and try again.

When is a story counted against my allowance? Only when the story is ready with its text and audio (and, for an illustrated story, its pictures). Unfinished or failed stories are not counted.

I didn’t like the story. You can reject it in the preview or create it again. If you see inappropriate content, tell us with “Report a problem”.

Can I listen without an internet connection? Yes. Open a story you have approved and save it to your device with “Download to listen offline”.

Does it keep playing with the screen off? Yes. In sleep mode the story keeps playing while the screen is locked and stops by itself when it ends.

Can I turn off the background sound? Yes. Use the “Soft background sound” switch while creating a story or in the player.

How do I cancel my subscription? With “Manage subscription” in Settings, or in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple Account, in Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; you need to cancel it separately.

I moved to a new phone and my subscription doesn’t show. Sign in with the same Apple Account and the same Liltly account (Apple or Google). If it still doesn’t show, write to us.

How do I delete my data? In Settings you can delete a single story, a child profile (with all its stories) or your whole account. Deletion is permanent.
