Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This is an English translation of our Turkish privacy policy. If the two differ, the Turkish version prevails. The legal bases below are those of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”). If you live in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada or Australia, see also “If you live outside Türkiye” below.

Who we are

Liltly is provided by Bahadır Çokdilli (the “data controller”). Address: Balkan Mahallesi, Nilüfer, Bursa, Türkiye. Contact: b.cokdilli@gmail.com.

Liltly is for parents. The parent creates the account, and the parent creates and approves the stories. Children do not create accounts in the app.

What data we collect

Parent account. When you sign in with Apple or Google: the account identifier, e-mail address and (if you share it) your name that these services give us.

Child profile. The nickname, age range and interests that you write, and, if you add one, the child’s favorite toy or object (for example “a white plush bunny called Snowy”); if you choose, the toy appears beside the child in their stories. We do not ask for or collect the child’s photo, voice, date of birth, school, address or location.

Stories. The topic, length, tone and notes you choose; the story text, pictures and audio that are produced; your approvals, rejections and problem reports. Short optional feedback you give about a story (for example “fell asleep”) is kept with the story and deleted when the story is deleted.

Where you heard about Liltly. After the first story we ask once, optionally (TikTok, Instagram, App Store search, a friend, other). If you answer, only the answer you chose is kept with your account and deleted when you delete your account. If you skip, nothing is sent.

Subscription. Your subscription status and purchase records. Your payment details (card and so on) never reach us; they are processed by Apple.

Ad campaign. If you downloaded the app from an Apple Ads advertisement, the campaign information Apple provides (which campaign and keyword) is matched with your account when you sign in. We use it only to measure whether our ads work. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, and we do not track you in other apps or websites.

Technical records. Request logs and app verification (App Check) data needed for the service to work and to prevent abuse.

We do not collect the advertising identifier, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

How we collect it. We collect data electronically, through the app: you write or choose most of it; account details come from Apple or Google sign-in, and technical records arrive automatically while the app is used.

What we use the data for

  • To create, store and deliver stories to you.
  • To manage your subscription and story allowance.
  • To check that content is suitable for children.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To understand where people hear about the app and plan our promotion accordingly (the “where did you hear” answer).
  • To measure whether our Apple Ads advertisements work (the ad campaign information).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete a story, a child profile or your account at any time in the app’s Settings. Deletion removes the records and files on the server, the copies on your device and your sign-in record.

Kept after an account is deleted:

  • Deletion record. The account’s number in our system (a random identifier that does not contain your name or e-mail), the deletion date, the counts of deleted records and the free-use counters. Empty records of story jobs whose content has been deleted (number and status) are also included. Purpose: to be able to show that the deletion was done and to stop delayed operations from writing data to a deleted account. Retention: 1 year from the account deletion.
  • Identity trace. If you used the free story, an irreversible (keyed hash) trace of your sign-in identity and the free-use counters. On its own this trace does not show who you are and it is not linked to your account number; it still counts as personal data and your rights above apply to it; it is used only so that the free story is not given a second time when an account is created again with the same identity. If you used nothing from the free allowance, no trace is kept. Retention: 2 years from the account deletion.

Records we must keep by law (for example financial records) are kept for the required periods.

Your rights

Under art. 11 of the KVKK you can apply to us to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in line with it,
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred,
  • ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected,
  • ask for the data to be deleted or destroyed under art. 7 of the KVKK,
  • ask for corrections and deletions to be notified to the third parties the data was transferred to,
  • object to a result against you that arises only from analysis by automated systems,
  • ask for compensation if you suffered damage because of unlawful processing.

How to apply. You can apply in writing (to our address), by registered electronic mail (KEP) or with a secure electronic signature, or from the e-mail address registered in the app to b.cokdilli@gmail.com. Include your name and surname, your contact address and your request. We conclude the application free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you do not find our answer sufficient, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu). For deletion, “Delete my account” in the app is also enough.

Children’s privacy

The app is meant for parents. As the child’s data we process only the nickname, age range, interests and favorite toy that you enter, based on the explicit consent you give as the parent or guardian. We recommend using a nickname instead of the real name. Notifications do not contain the child’s name.

Security

Only the signed-in account holder can access the data, through our server. Direct access to the database and files is closed. Stories you download are not included in device backups.

If you live outside Türkiye

Liltly is offered from Türkiye. If you live in one of the countries below, the following applies in addition to the sections above. Where the law of your country gives you more protection, that protection applies.

United States

Children’s privacy. The app is for parents. Children do not create accounts, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child. The only information about a child is what the parent enters: a nickname, an age range, interests and, optionally, a favorite toy. Before the first child profile is saved, the parent must tick a consent box in the app. As the parent you can, at any time and free of charge:

  • see the child’s information in Settings,
  • correct it by editing the child profile,
  • stop any further collection and have it deleted by deleting the child profile or your account.

We do not use children’s information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes. The service providers in “Who we share data with” receive it only to make the story. If you believe we hold a child’s information without a parent’s consent, write to us and we will delete it.

State privacy rights (for example California). We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can ask to know what personal information we hold about you, to correct it and to delete it, and we will not treat you differently for asking. Send your request to b.cokdilli@gmail.com from the e-mail address registered in the app; an authorised agent can make it for you with your written permission. We answer within 45 days.

United Kingdom

We process your data under the UK GDPR. The legal bases are: performing our contract with you (article 6(1)(b)) for the account, stories and subscription; your consent as the parent (article 6(1)(a)) for the child profile; our legitimate interests (article 6(1)(f)) for content checks, security, the “where did you hear” answer, Apple Ads measurement and the record kept after deletion; and our legal obligations (article 6(1)(c)) for financial records.

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable form and withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawal does not affect earlier processing). We answer within one month. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

The app does not show ads, does not track the user in other apps, does not collect location and does not profile children.

Transfers. Our providers are in Canada and the United States. Canada is recognised by the UK as giving adequate protection. For providers in the United States we rely on the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider takes part in it, and otherwise on the safeguards in the provider’s data processing terms.

Canada

You can ask to access and correct your personal information and withdraw your consent, subject to legal and contractual limits; we answer within 30 days. The database, files and server are in Canada (Montréal); the other providers are in the United States, where your data may be accessed by authorities under local law. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

Australia

You can ask to access and correct your personal information; we answer within 30 days. Your data is disclosed to providers in Canada and the United States, as described above. If you have a complaint, write to us first; if you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
